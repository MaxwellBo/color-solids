sRGB solid in Oklab

Every color an sRGB display can produce, set down at its Oklab coordinate. Lightness is up. Green–red and blue–yellow run across the floor, on the same scale.

The top of the solid is cut away at this lightness.

  • Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, R resets the view
  • Up and down arrows move the cut. F switches to the full solid
  • In a headset the left controller holds the solid, lightness along its aim. Hold the right trigger to lay a level plane from the tip: below stays solid, above keeps only its edges. Release to close the cut.
  • The left stick walks. Flick the right stick sideways to turn.
